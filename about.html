Salary vs Inflation

Does your raise actually beat inflation?

About SalaryVsInflation.com

SalaryVsInflation.com is a free tool that compares your salary raise against the current inflation rate. It shows whether your purchasing power actually increases after a raise — or whether rising prices erase the gain.

This calculator uses a straightforward formula: it adjusts your new salary by the inflation rate to reveal the real value of your income. The result tells you whether you're genuinely ahead, just breaking even, or effectively taking a pay cut despite a nominal increase.

Who this is for

Anyone evaluating a raise, job offer, or cost-of-living adjustment. If you've ever wondered whether your salary increase actually means more money in your pocket, this tool answers that directly.

How it works

Enter your current salary, your raise percentage, and the inflation rate. The calculator computes your new nominal salary, adjusts it for inflation, and shows the real change in dollars and percentage terms.
